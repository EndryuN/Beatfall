# Beatfall — "Song → Playable Level" video prompt (15s)

Two forms of the same prompt: a single-paragraph version for tools that want one block,
and the full structured version for keyframing or trimming.

---

## Single-paragraph prompt

15-second seamless morph, premium neon rhythm-game motion graphics on a deep purple-black
background with soft glow, bloom, and floating dust; four recurring accent colors — hot pink
(drums), electric blue (bass), warm gold (vocals), mint-green (lead). Open on a lone luminous white
audio waveform drawing across black (0–2s); it delaminates into four stacked colored instrument
waveform ribbons, each pulsing to its own rhythm (2–4s); vertical beat-grid lines sweep in and lock
on the beat while drum transients flash, the gold vocal wave condenses into a rising-and-falling
string of glowing pitch-note orbs, and a violin/piano emblem ignites to classify the lead (4–7s);
the flat ribbons rotate into 3D as four vertical neon lanes receding in perspective and the notes
snap onto them as colored circles falling toward a glowing hit-line, briefly fanning Easy/Medium/Hard
difficulty cards (7–10s); the camera pulls back to reveal the full playable game — falling-note
highway on the left, a music-video panel on the right, cute cartoon band (drummer at a kit,
violinist) bouncing below, score and combo climbing — and notes are struck with ring-shockwaves and
particle bursts, ending on a gold flash, confetti, and a giant glowing "S" grade stamping
center-frame (10–15s). Smooth cinematic camera, shallow depth of field, motion blur on streaking
notes, beat-synced pulses; audio splits into four soloed instrument stems then resolves into a
punchy full track with arcade hit-pings and a final cheer. No real human faces, no logos, no
readable paragraphs of text, no cluttered HUD, no glitchy datamosh, no strobing, no watermark.

---

## Full structured prompt

### Logline
In fifteen seconds, a single glowing audio waveform is split into four colored instrument layers,
scanned for beats and melody, and morphed into a neon four-lane rhythm-game highway with notes
cascading toward a glowing hit-line — the birth of a playable level, shown as one seamless,
accelerating transformation rather than separate cuts.

### Style bible (apply throughout)
A premium music-app / rhythm-game aesthetic. Deep purple-to-black gradient background, faint
volumetric haze, floating dust motes catching light. Everything emits soft neon glow with gentle
bloom. The signature palette is exactly four accent colors that recur as a motif because they
represent the four instrument stems: hot pink (drums), electric blue (bass), warm gold (vocals),
mint-green (lead/other). Surfaces are dark glass with thin 1px luminous edges. Motion is fluid,
weighty, confident — eases in and out, never snaps harshly except on musical beats, where it pulses
crisply. The piece feels like watching sound become structure: organic at the start (flowing
waveforms), increasingly geometric and grid-locked toward the end (lanes, notes, beat lines).
Cinematic camera, shallow depth of field, tasteful motion blur. High-end motion-graphics, 60fps
feel, clean anti-aliasing, no clutter — Apple-keynote polish crossed with a neon arcade.

### The single continuous idea
No hard cuts. The 15 seconds is one unbroken morph: a lone waveform peels into four ribbons, those
ribbons sprout beat-lines and notes, the notes tip forward into perspective and fall down four lanes,
and the camera pulls back to reveal the finished game. Each stage flows into the next as a
transformation of the same material, so the music literally turns into a game.

### Second-by-second breakdown
- **0:00–0:02 — Raw sound.** Black frame; a single thin luminous white waveform draws left-to-right,
  jittering to unheard music. Scanlines, soft vignette, slow push-in. Label "AUDIO" fades in/out.
  Low bass hum builds.
- **0:02–0:04 — Separation (stems).** The white waveform delaminates into four stacked parallel
  ribbons sliding into their own lanes, each igniting its instrument color and moving with its own
  rhythm: pink drums punch, blue bass rolls, gold vocals undulate, green lead spikes. Tiny icons
  (drum, bass clef, mic, guitar/violin) fade in. Audio splits into four soloed elements.
- **0:04–0:07 — Analysis (beats, notes, instrument).** Vertical beat-grid lines sweep across all
  four ribbons and lock, pulsing on the beat. Pink drum transients flash and tag kick/snare/hat as a
  scan-line passes. The gold vocal wave condenses into a connected string of glowing pitch-note orbs
  rising and falling. A reticle sweeps the green lead and a violin/piano emblem ignites with a chime.
  Label "ANALYZING."
- **0:07–0:10 — Generation (the chart).** The flat ribbons rotate toward camera and reform as four
  vertical neon lanes receding in perspective. Melody orbs and drum hits snap onto the lanes as
  colored circles aligned to the beat-grid, scrolling down toward a bright hit-line; long holds are
  elongated trails. Three difficulty cards EASY/MEDIUM/HARD fan out (each denser) then collapse back
  to the live highway. Label "GENERATING."
- **0:10–0:15 — Reveal + payoff.** Camera pulls back to the full game: neon highway left, music-video
  panel right, cartoon band (drummer at a kit, violinist, bassist, singer) bouncing below, score and
  combo climbing. Notes are struck at the hit-line with ring-shockwaves and colored particle bursts;
  the band cheers; on the final beat one note lands center and the screen blooms gold with confetti
  and a giant glowing "S" grade stamps in. Hold the final composed shot.

### Camera & motion
Intimate push-in on the waveform; stable with micro-drift through split and analysis so transforms
read; slight arc as ribbons rotate into 3D lanes (0:07); smooth dolly-back for the reveal (0:10).
Shallow DOF, focal plane on the active element, motion blur on streaking notes/particles. Only
beat-synced motions snap; everything else eases. Energy: calm and curious → analytical tension →
kinetic joyful gameplay.

### Color & lighting
Deep purple-black radial gradient, darkest at corners, warm glow behind the active element. The four
stem colors are the through-line — introduced at the split, recurring in lanes, notes, hit-bursts,
and band. Emissive light: glows, blooms, neon edges, flare sparks on impacts. Rich blacks, subtle
chromatic shimmer on bright edges. Final frame is the most saturated, luminous moment.

### On-screen labels (minimal)
At most four tiny elegant lower-third labels in a thin glowing sans-serif: "AUDIO" (0:01),
"SEPARATING INSTRUMENTS" (0:03), "ANALYZING" (0:05), "GENERATING" (0:08). The only large type is the
final grade "S." Don't crowd the frame.

### Sound design
0:00 building hum + faint full-band loop. 0:02 mix splits into four panned soloed elements. 0:04 soft
scan sweep + beat-locked ticks. 0:07 rising synth riser. 0:10 full punchy track; each hit a crisp
arcade ping; 0:14 impact + cheer + shimmer for the S-grade.

### Negative prompt
No real human faces, no realistic photographic people, no logos/brand marks, no readable paragraphs
of text, no cluttered HUD, no generic "digital network/blockchain" tropes, no glitchy datamosh, no
muddy gray backgrounds, no strobing/seizure-risk flashing, no watermark, no warped UI text, no extra
fingers/hands on the cartoon band, no low-contrast color, no slow boring pacing.

### Final frame
Full game composition: neon four-lane highway mid-cascade with a fresh hit-line particle burst, the
music-video panel glowing right, the cartoon band cheering below, the combo counter lit, and a large
radiant gold "S" stamped center-frame — "a song just became a game."
