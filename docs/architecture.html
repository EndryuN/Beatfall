Beatfall — system architecture native Windows service

The game (browser) talks to mlib — a local service on the GPU desktop, split into a thin API process and a GPU worker joined by a durable job queue. Audio is touched only during analysis, then deleted; at play time video and sound stream from the official YouTube embed.
